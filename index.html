About

I am a Ph.D. student at the KAIST Graduate School of AI, working in the Bio Imaging, Signal Processing & Learning Lab (BISPL) under the supervision of Prof. Jong Chul Ye.

My research focuses on generative models (3D/4D, videos) and robotics: how the dynamic world looks, how it changes over time, and how agents act in it.

Previously I was a Research Intern at NAVER AI Lab (Generation Research Team), hosted by Jin-Hwa Kim.
