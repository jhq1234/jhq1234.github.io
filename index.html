About

I am a Ph.D. student at the KAIST Graduate School of AI, working in the Bio Imaging, Signal Processing & Learning Lab (BISPL) under the supervision of Prof. Jong Chul Ye.

My research lives at the intersection of 3D/4D scene generation and world models — building generative systems that reason about scenes across space and time. I am especially interested in coaxing 4D structure and long-horizon video out of off-the-shelf diffusion models, largely without additional training.

Previously I was a Research Intern at NAVER AI Lab (Generation Research Team), hosted by Jin-Hwa Kim.
